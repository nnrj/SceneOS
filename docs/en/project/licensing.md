# Licensing policy

SceneOS-owned source is released under the MIT License. The goal is to reduce
adoption friction for the operating system, drivers, SDKs, libraries,
applications, and commercial products. Authors of applications, drivers, and
independent libraries retain their choice of license.

Third-party components keep their own licenses; building or loading them does
not relicense them as MIT. The repository-root `THIRD_PARTY.md` records each
component, purpose, license, and distribution method.

NNOS remains a historical and behavioural reference. SceneOS does not copy the
NNOS source tree, keeping implementation provenance and license boundaries
clear.
